Ms. Min Yee CHIN is currently pursuing a PhD in the Carbon Neutrality and Climate Change Thrust at the Hong Kong University Science and Technology (Guangzhou) under the supervision of Prof. Vincent Woon Kok Sin. She has been working with Prof. Vincent since her undergraduate final year project at Xiamen University Malaysia, continuing through her Master’s degree, and now into doctoral research. Min Yee’s current research focuses on carbon forecasting and optimization, with a particular emphasis on the clean energy transition in Global South. Her work aims to enhance understanding of carbon dynamics and inform sustainable development strategies. During her undergraduate and Master’s studies, she specialized in Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) and Life Cycle Sustainability Assessment (LCSA) in the context of waste management. Building on this foundation, she seeks to integrate life cycle approaches with carbon modeling to address pressing environmental challenges. Min Yee is passionate about advancing research that supports evidence-based climate action and equitable sustainability transitions.
