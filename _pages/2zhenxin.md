Ms. Zhen Xin PHUANG obtained her doctoral degree from the New Energy Science and Engineering programme of Xiamen University Malaysia (XMUM) in 2025. Started her research journey since 2021, Phuang exhibited strong research dedication in sustainable waste circularity management and waste-related climate solutions. Harnessing cross-disciplinary research skills (i.e., life cycle assessment, system optimization, and geographical information system), Phuang specializes in modelling scientifically-backed optimal solutions for actionable circular plastics economy policymaking. Until 2025, Phuang has accumulated an h-index of 10, and published 15 research articles (in which 12 papers were published in SCI-indexed Q1 journals with impact factor ranging from 6.082 to 13.2). Among the publications, Phuang claimed first authorship of 7 papers and established professional collaboration with internationally renowned universities (University of Oxford, Cornell University, Beijing Normal University). Phuang has received three ‘Best Presenter Award’ from prestigious international conferences (e.g., ICLCA conference series) and was the first-prize winner of the XMUM 3MT Competition 2025. Being listed as one of the 22 semi-finalists of the Merdeka Award Grant for International Attachment 2025 (shortlisting rate <10%) supported by government-linked companies, Phuang demonstrated her competence as a young researcher at national level. Apart from research activities, Phuang also committed to teaching assistantship and research assistant guidance (i.e., guided 12 final-year students and graduate research assistants to date).
